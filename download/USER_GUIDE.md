# User Guide — AI Content Engine

A complete walkthrough for a new user. From zero to your first published
Facebook post.

---

## What this system does

You give it photos and a one-sentence brief. The AI:

1. **Analyses** each photo (vision model identifies subject, setting, mood)
2. **Plans** a 7-day content strategy (which content pillars, which days)
3. **Writes** 7-14 posts (3 candidates per slot, picks the best)
4. **Schedules** them across the week at optimal times
5. **Publishes** them automatically to your Facebook Page
6. **Tracks** engagement and learns what works

You review and approve everything before it goes live.

---

## The 10 sections (left sidebar)

### 1. Dashboard

Home screen. Shows this week's campaign, KPI tiles (content supplied, posts
generated, scheduled, awaiting approval), and preview lists of recent activity.

### 2. Create Week

**Step 1 — Upload:** drag and drop photos (or click to browse). Up to 10 files.

**Step 2 — Brief & generate:** write a one-sentence brief, select objectives
(Awareness, Engagement, Sales, Leads), pick a campaign, click Generate.

The AI then: analyses photos → generates strategy → writes 3 candidates per
day → evaluates each → picks the best → creates pending posts. Takes 1-4 min.

### 3. Approval

All pending posts grouped by day (Mon→Sun). Each shows photo, hook, caption,
CTA, hashtags, badges. Actions: **Edit**, **Regenerate** (with instruction),
**Approve**. Bottom: **Approve all (N)** for bulk approval.

### 4. Calendar

14-day grid of scheduled/published posts. Click any post for details.
Filters: All / Scheduled / Published / Pending.

### 5. Campaigns

Campaign cards with content mix bar chart, date range, post count, engagement.
Click to expand and see posts.

### 6. Analytics

KPI tiles (reach, engagement, rate, published). Bar charts by pillar and
objective. Top 5 posts. AI insights from the Learning Engine.

### 7. Brand Brain

Edit the persistent brand profile: name, description, audience, personality,
writing style, CTA. Vocabulary lists (Use/Avoid). Brand rules. Products.
This is fed into every AI call.

### 8. Assistant

Chat with the workspace AI. It knows your campaigns, posts, and analytics.
Ask: "What performed best?" or "Make Friday's caption less salesy."

### 9. Distribution

Connect Facebook Pages via OAuth ("Connect with Facebook") or manually.
Each account shows its destinations. Add/Edit/Remove destinations. Add
Groups with Manual automation.

### 10. Settings

- **Workspace profile** — change name, upload custom logo
- **Approval mode** — Manual / Assisted / Autonomous
- **Recycle bin** — generate fresh variations of old top posts
- **Audit log** — every action recorded

## The weekly workflow (3 minutes/week)

### Monday morning

1. **Create Week** → upload photos → write brief → Generate
2. Wait 1-4 minutes
3. **Approval** → review, edit/regenerate as needed → Approve all
4. Done. Scheduler publishes throughout the week.

### During the week

- Check **Calendar** for what's coming
- Check **Analytics** for performance
- Chat with **Assistant** for ideas

### End of week

- Review **Analytics** for the week
- **Learning Engine** gives insights + recommended mix for next week
- Recycle top performers in **Settings → Recycle bin**

## Tips

- **Photos:** high-quality, well-lit, clear subject. Mix product/lifestyle/people.
- **Brief:** be specific. "New summer collection arriving Friday. Drive store visits, not online sales. Don't make every post a sales pitch."
- **Brand Brain:** fill it in thoroughly. The vocabulary lists are powerful.
- **Objectives:** pick 1-2 per week. Awareness + Engagement is a good default.

---

## FAQ

**How long does generation take?** 1-4 minutes for 7 posts.

**What if I don't like a post?** Regenerate (tell AI what to change) or Edit manually.

**Can I schedule specific times?** Yes — edit the post in Approval or Calendar.

**What if publish fails?** Auto-retry with backoff (1m, 5m, 15m, 60m). See failed posts in Dashboard.

**Multiple Facebook Pages?** Yes — connect each via OAuth. Assign posts to different Pages.

**Facebook Groups?** Yes — add as destination with Manual automation. AI adapts tone per group.
