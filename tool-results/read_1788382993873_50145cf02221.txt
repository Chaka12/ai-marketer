   599→13. Facebook should be an adapter
   600→
   601→This is important for modularity.
   602→
   603→Don't hard-code Facebook throughout your application.
   604→
   605→Create a generic publishing interface:
   606→
   607→Publisher
   608→│
   609→├── FacebookPublisher
   610→├── InstagramPublisher
   611→├── TikTokPublisher
   612→├── XPublisher
   613→└── LinkedInPublisher
   614→
   615→Then your application says:
   616→
   617→Publish this content.
   618→
   619→The Facebook adapter knows how Facebook works.
   620→
   621→That means you can add another platform later without rebuilding the entire application.
   622→
   623→Meta's official developer ecosystem provides APIs for Page-related operations, but the exact permissions and capabilities available to an app depend on Meta's current platform policies and review requirements. So the Facebook adapter should isolate those assumptions rather than spreading them throughout your codebase.
